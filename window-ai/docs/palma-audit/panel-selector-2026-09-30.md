# Palma Panel Selector snapshot — 2026-09-30

Source: https://www.palmadoor.com/panel-selector (3 pages, 216 entries), plus each panel's page for "Available panel sizes" and "suitable glass sizes".

How to read this:
- **Widths** come from the panel page. Where the selector card listed fewer widths, that is noted.
- **Glass** is the panel page's "suitable glass sizes". "none listed" means the page showed no glass. Several pages list none even where the price book prices glass (e.g. WG00, WG34), so treat the glass lists as **indicative, not exhaustive**.
- **MP-FG**: the page says "Multipoint locks are necessary for all Fiberglass doors."
- **MP-8'**: the page says "Multipoint locks are necessary for all 8' doors."
